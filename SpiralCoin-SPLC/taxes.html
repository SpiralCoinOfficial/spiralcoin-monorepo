Tax Method: FIFOFirst In, First Out cost basis
The FIFO (First In, First Out) method is used to calculate cost basis. Your first-purchased coins are treated as the first sold. This is the default method recommended by the IRS for cryptocurrency. Short-term gains (held <1 year) are taxed as ordinary income. Long-term gains (held >1 year) qualify for reduced capital gains rates (0%, 15%, or 20% based on your bracket).
