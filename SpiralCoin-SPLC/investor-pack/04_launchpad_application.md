1. What is the long-term token utility beyond speculation?
2. Why should retail investors buy SPLC vs. existing alternatives?

6. How will you defend against the inevitable post-IDO sell pressure?

Prepare written answers in advance.

## Application strategy

Apply to **all four in parallel**. They won't compete — they all want exclusivity but rarely
require it. Whoever accepts first gets your IDO. If multiple accept, negotiate the best terms
(highest valuation, lowest vesting cliff, highest community quality).

## Application timeline

| Week | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Submit applications, deliver pitch deck + audit + KYC docs |
| 2–3 | Initial screening calls (expect 30-min Zoom each) |
| 4–6 | Due diligence, contract review, legal review |
| 7 | Offer terms negotiation |
| 8 | Signed agreement, IDO date set 2–6 weeks out |
| 9–14 | Marketing ramp, whitelist registration, anticipation builds |
| 15 | IDO live |

## Honest costs

| Item | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Audit (mandatory) | $5,000 – $25,000 |
| KYC service (mandatory) | $200 – $1,000 |
| Launchpad listing fee | Usually $0 (they take % of raise) |
| Launchpad token allocation | 1% – 5% of total supply, typically vested |
| Legal review of terms | $2,000 – $10,000 |
| Marketing during IDO ramp | $10,000 – $100,000 |
