## Recommended outreach order

1. **Tier 3 first** (angels, syndicates) — fast nos teach you the deck, fast yeses build momentum
2. **Tier 2** (smaller funds) — most likely to invest at your stage, take meetings
3. **Tier 4** (chain ecosystem funds) — almost-guaranteed grant/investment if you deploy on their chain
4. **Tier 1 last** (big names) — they want to see traction + early commitments before they engage

Don't start with Multicoin. Start with Bankless Ventures and Robot Ventures. Earn your way up.

## What you need before you send the first email

- [ ] Audited contracts (CertiK, Hacken, or OpenZeppelin)
- [ ] Tokenomics one-pager (`01_tokenomics_onepager.md`)
- [ ] Pitch deck PDF (built from `02_pitch_deck_outline.md`)
- [ ] Founder KYC published (LinkedIn at minimum, ideally launchpad-verified dox)
- [ ] Live product at <www.spiralcoin.net> (you have this)
- [ ] Data room (Notion or Google Drive folder, shared link)
- [ ] Calendly link for booking calls
- [ ] At least one verifiable traction metric (real users, real revenue, real community)
- [ ] Legal: confirmed structure (Reg D 506(c) entity, foreign foundation, etc.) — talk to lawyer

---

## What to NEVER do in outreach

- Don't claim to be backed by funds that haven't committed
- Don't fabricate audit completion
- Don't promise specific returns
- Don't mass-email — every email personalized
- Don't email US retail investors with token offers (SEC violation)
- Don't ignore replies — even a "no" deserves a thank-you
- Don't take more than 24 hours to reply to a warm intro
