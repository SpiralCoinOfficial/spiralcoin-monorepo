# VC Outreach — Cold Email Templates + Sequence

> Trading involves risk. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Send these as plain text — not HTML. Personalize every send. VCs ignore mass emails.

---

## Subject line options (A/B test)

- "SpiralCoin — multi-chain market intelligence, raising $500k seed"
- "Intro: SpiralCoin (live product, audited, raising seed)"
- "Quick intro — SpiralCoin / SPLC governance token"

Keep subject under 50 characters. No emojis. No clickbait.

## Reply handlers

### "We don't invest at this stage"

```
Understood — thanks for the quick response. We'll keep you on quarterly
updates and revisit at Series A. Best of luck with the rest of your portfolio.
```

### "Send me the deck"

```
Attached: pitch deck and tokenomics one-pager. Audit report and contract
addresses in the data room here: [link]. Happy to schedule a deeper dive call
if helpful.
```

### "What's the discount vs IDO?"

```
Seed round: 30-50% discount to IDO price (negotiable based on check size and
vesting acceptance). Floor: 6-month cliff + 18-month linear. Larger checks
($100k+) can negotiate price; smaller checks take SAFT at the floor terms.
```

### "Who else is in?"

If nobody — be honest:

```
You'd be among the first. We're talking to [N] other funds in parallel. I can
share specific names under NDA on a call.
```

If someone has committed:

```
[Name of committed investor with permission] is in for $X. We have term sheets
out to [N] others.
```

**Never lie about other investors. It always gets discovered and kills the round.**

## What to never do

- Don't promise specific returns ("guaranteed 10x")
- Don't use urgency tactics ("closing tomorrow!")
- Don't fabricate traction, audits, or partnerships
- Don't send to US-based retail investors via these emails (Reg D 506(c) only allows
  general solicitation to verified accredited investors)
- Don't ignore compliance — the SEC reads emails
