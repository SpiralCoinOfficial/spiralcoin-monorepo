01 · The Honest PitchWhy we're funding the boring way.

Most 2026 token launches still open at multi-million-dollar pre-set valuations, then chase price-target marketing. SpiralCoin does the opposite:

  • 1,000,000,000 SPLC fixed supply, deployed once, no mint function post-init.
  • 9-bucket allocation locked on-chain via vesting, staking, LP-lock, and multisig-bound treasury contracts.
  • Bootstrap public liquidity: 1,000,000 SPLC + $1,000 USDC in a Uniswap V3 1% full-range pool — LP NFT locked 12 months in SPLCLPLock.sol.
  • No private round priced above retail. No promised returns. No fabricated FDV. If the project is real, market demand prices it.
