## Title

SpiralCoin — fair-launch ERC-20 with native CoW Swap routability on Arbitrum

## Summary

Seeking $20,000 USDC to seed the SPLC/USDC AMM pool on Uniswap V3 (Arbitrum One,
1% fee tier, full-range, 12-month LP lock). Once the pool has depth, SPLC
becomes routable through CoW Swap's solver competition, giving CoW users a new
fair-launch token to swap with MEV protection.

## Why CoW DAO should care

1. **A new fair-launch token routes through CoW solvers** — every SPLC swap on
   CoW generates protocol fees and improves solver competition data.
2. **No founder pre-mine / no insider allocations** — SPLC is exactly the kind
   of project CoW community has historically supported.
3. **Fixed-supply commitment** — no inflation, no validator emission. The 3.14%
   transfer tax is the only operational funding stream and is immutable in source.
4. **MEV protection matters for SPLC users** — small-cap tokens are sandwich-bait
   on naive AMM routers. CoW's batch auctions are a natural fit for SPLC
   liquidity and we'd commit to recommending CoW as the default swap interface.
