## 4. Red flags — walk away immediately

If any of these appear during the application process, **do not proceed and do not pay
anything**:

- **Any request for upfront fees.** Legitimate grant programs never charge an
  "application fee," "gas fee," "smart-contract activation fee," "KYC fee," or
  "release fee." Every single instance of this is a scam.
- **Any request for your seed phrase or private key.** Never. Under any circumstance.
- **DM offers from "grant team members"** on Telegram / Discord / X who reach out to
  you first. Real grant teams do not cold-DM applicants. Verify identities on the
  official program site only.
- **Lookalike URLs** — e.g. `base-grants.xyz`, `arbitrumfoundation-grants.org`,
  `mantle-ecofund.io`. Always navigate from the parent project's verified site
  (base.org, arbitrum.foundation, mantle.xyz, optimism.io, polygon.technology,
  gitcoin.co, conduit.xyz). Do not click grant links sent via DM.
- **"Guaranteed approval"** services that promise to write or submit your application
  for a cut of the grant. Real grant teams read every application directly.
- **Requests to connect your wallet to "claim" a grant.** Approvals are paid via
  invoice or to an address *you provide*, never via a wallet-connect transaction you
  sign. If a "claim" site asks for a signature on something other than a plain
  message login, walk away.
- **Pressure tactics** — "decide in 24 hours," "limited slots," "exclusive cohort."
  Real programs run on documented schedules.
