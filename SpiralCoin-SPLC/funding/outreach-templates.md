## 8. Target-hunting guide (find 20 real handles in 20 minutes)

Sources, ranked by hit-rate:

1. **Arbitrum Foundation grants page** — every funded team has founder X handles. The teams that got grants ≥ 6 months ago have runway questions of their own, so they sometimes pay attention to peer projects.
   <https://arbitrum.foundation/grants>

2. **DefiLlama → Arbitrum → sort by TVL ascending, filter to small protocols** — small Arbitrum DeFi founders are reachable, friendly, and often hold treasury USDC. Click through to their X handle.
   <https://defillama.com/chain/Arbitrum>

3. **GeckoTerminal trending Arbitrum pools** — look at who's deploying right now. Trade activity in V3 pools surfaces active LPs and angels.
   <https://www.geckoterminal.com/arbitrum/pools>

4. **X advanced search** — these queries find people *actively* allocating capital, not just talkers:
   - `min_faves:50 (sponsoring OR seeded OR LP) arbitrum from:[date]`
   - `(angel investing OR backed) arbitrum dapp min_faves:100`
   - `building on arbitrum -is:retweet min_replies:20`

5. **GitHub** — stars + contributors on `OffchainLabs/arbitrum-orbit-sdk`, `Uniswap/v3-core`, and any Arbitrum bridge repo. Active devs become angels.

6. **CT Telegram / Discord groups** — Arbitrum Hub, Camelot DEX, LayerZero builder channels. Read pinned messages for who runs them; those are the connectors.

### Pacing (so X doesn't shadowban you)

- ≤ **15 cold DMs per day** from a single account
- Wait ≥ 60 seconds between sends
- Don't send the same link in 2 messages in a row — vary the UTM (`seed_pool_arb_builder`, `seed_pool_angel`, etc.) so the URL is unique
- If you get auto-blocked, wait 24h before resuming

## UTM cheat sheet

| Channel | utm_source | utm_medium |
|---------|------------|------------|
| Cold email | `email` | `cold` |
| Follow-up email | `email` | `followup` |
| X / Twitter DM | `x` | `dm` |
| X / Twitter post | `x` | `post` |
| Telegram group | `telegram` | `group` |
| Discord | `discord` | `dm` or `channel` |
| LinkedIn DM | `linkedin` | `dm` |
| Podcast mention | `podcast` | `mention` |

Always set `utm_campaign=seed_pool_20k` (or `whitepaper`, `splc_listing`, etc.).

GA4 will surface these under **Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition**,
and `utm-capture.js` persists the first-touch attribution in `localStorage` for
30 days so signup conversions are credited to the originating channel.
