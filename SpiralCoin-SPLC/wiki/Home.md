# SpiralCoin Wiki

Welcome to the SpiralCoin (SPLC) project wiki. SpiralCoin is a regulated, transparent
crypto trading and infrastructure platform under build by SpiralCoin Official.
Production site: **<https://www.spiralcoin.net>**

> ⚠️ **Status:** Pre-launch / public preview. Trading interfaces are demo only.
> The SPLC token is not yet deployed to mainnet. Sponsor contributions are
> donations toward bootstrapping public on-chain infrastructure — they are
> not investments, equity, or revenue-share entitlements.
